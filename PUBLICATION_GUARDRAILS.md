# Interpretation guardrails

The following distinctions are part of the scientific interpretation of
this release.

## Analytical response versus concentration

Corrected workbook values are internal-standard-normalized
CPS-derived responses. They are not presented as reconstructed ng/mL
participant concentrations.

## Clinical-group reconstruction

Participant-level clinical labels were not directly identified in the
deposited workbook. Two stable source-order blocks were reconstructed
against baseline aggregate patterns reported in the source publication.

Any group-dependent analysis is therefore conditional on that
reconstruction.

## Causal interpretation

Treatment and longitudinal time are not separated experimentally in the
hypercholesterolaemic source block. The reanalysis is not presented as a
new randomized causal estimate of simvastatin treatment.

## Statistical departure

The control-baseline reference in the Mahalanobis analysis is a
statistical reference architecture. Movement toward it is not described
as restoration of physiological homeostasis.

## Network localization

The supported 27-OHC--7beta-OHC rank-association change is a localized
component of global rank-dependence rewiring. It is not interpreted as
a direct biochemical interaction.
