## Measurement-scale limitation

The deposited workbook contains instrument counts per second and
internal-standard-normalized CPS-derived analytical responses. The
analyte-specific calibration functions required to reconstruct
participant-level absolute concentrations are not present in the
deposited workbook.

Accordingly, this project does not relabel the corrected analytical
responses as ng/mL concentrations.
