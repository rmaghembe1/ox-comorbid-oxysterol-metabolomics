OX-COMORBID G2A R2T S1 INTERPRETATION GUARDRAILS

S1 PURPOSE
Quantify longitudinal change in multivariate statistical departure from the reconstructed control-baseline response architecture.

REFERENCE
The reference is the ten reconstructed control subjects at baseline.
It is a statistical reference distribution, not a normative definition of biological homeostasis.

COORDINATES
All 40 observations are mapped analyte-wise into pooled empirical rank-normal coordinates.
This removes analyte-specific response scale while preserving monotone ordering.

COVARIANCE
The five-dimensional control-baseline covariance is regularized using the deterministic Oracle Approximating Shrinkage estimator.
The OAS covariance uses the maximum-likelihood n denominator.

DEPARTURE
D2 denotes squared Mahalanobis departure in rank-normal response space.
Delta D2 equals D2 at B minus D2 at A.
Negative mean Delta D2 means movement toward the statistical reference.
Positive mean Delta D2 means movement away from the statistical reference.

INFERENCE
The primary S1 statistic is absolute mean Delta D2.
The sign is descriptive orientation and is not separately selected or tested.
Exact inference enumerates all 1024 hyper-subject whole-vector A/B swaps.

PROVENANCE
Clinical-group identity is PROVISIONAL_INDIRECT_HIGH_CONFIDENCE.
The control-baseline reference uses a group identity reconstructed partly from baseline aggregate oxysterol patterns.
Therefore S1 is provenance-sensitive and not an independent validation of group identity.
The exact p-value does not incorporate uncertainty in group reconstruction.

PROHIBITED LANGUAGE
Do not call reduced D2 restoration of homeostasis.
Do not call reduced D2 normalization of biology.
Do not claim causal simvastatin effects.
Do not interpret D2 as absolute concentration distance.
