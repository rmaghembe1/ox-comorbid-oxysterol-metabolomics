OX-COMORBID G2A R2S S2 INTERPRETATION BOUNDARIES

ANALYSIS
S2 compares five-dimensional longitudinal rank-displacement architecture between the reconstructed hypercholesterolaemic and control source blocks.

MEASUREMENT
The statistic is calibration-robust and uses within-timepoint ranks.
It does not use participant ng/mL concentrations.

PERMUTATION INFERENCE
All C(20,10)=184756 possible 10-versus-10 subject-label allocations are enumerated.
The reported p-value is a complete-enumeration label-permutation reference under exchangeability.
It is not design-based randomization inference because clinical groups were not randomized.

PROVENANCE SENSITIVITY
Clinical-group identity is PROVISIONAL_INDIRECT_HIGH_CONFIDENCE.
The group mapping was reconstructed using published baseline aggregate patterns from these five analytes.
Baseline ranks also enter the S2 displacement through rank_B-minus-rank_A.
Therefore S2 is not an independent validation of the reconstructed group labels.
Its inferential interpretation is conditional on the reconstructed mapping.
Follow-up values were not used to establish the group mapping.

CLAIM
A supported S2 result means longitudinal rank-displacement architecture differs between the reconstructed clinical blocks under the specified permutation reference.

PROHIBITED CLAIMS
Do not call S2 a randomized treatment effect.
Do not attribute the difference causally to simvastatin.
Do not interpret rank displacement as absolute concentration change.
Do not treat the S2 p-value as correcting uncertainty in the reconstructed group mapping.
