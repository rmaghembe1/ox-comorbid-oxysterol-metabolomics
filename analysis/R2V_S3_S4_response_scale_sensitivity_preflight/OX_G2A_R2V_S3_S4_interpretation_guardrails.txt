OX-COMORBID G2A R2V S3/S4 INTERPRETATION GUARDRAILS

PURPOSE
S3 and S4 are secondary response-scale sensitivity analyses.
They test whether conclusions remain visible when using the deposited normalized analytical-response magnitudes.

VALUE SCALE
The input is internal-standard-normalized CPS-derived analytical response.
Subject response is natural log(response_B / response_A).
This is a log analytical-response ratio.
It is not an oxysterol concentration fold-change.

S3
Hypercholesterolaemic and control groups are analyzed separately.
Each analyte uses absolute mean log response as its exact-test statistic.
Signed mean log response gives direction.
Median, Q1, Q3 and IQR are descriptive robust summaries.
Each group uses a complete 2^10 subject-level A/B swap universe.
Holm correction is applied separately across five analytes in each group.

S4
For each analyte, S4 compares mean subject log response between reconstructed clinical blocks.
All C(20,10)=184756 complete-subject label allocations are enumerated.
Holm correction is applied across the five S4 analytes.

PROVENANCE
Clinical-group mapping remains PROVISIONAL_INDIRECT_HIGH_CONFIDENCE.
Baseline oxysterol patterns contributed to reconstruction of group identity.
Because the log response includes baseline in its denominator, S4 is not independent validation of reconstructed group identity.
S4 inference is conditional on that mapping.

CAUSALITY
Clinical group and intervention exposure are inseparable.
No S3 or S4 result establishes a randomized or causal simvastatin effect.

ABSOLUTE CONCENTRATION
No S3 or S4 statistic may be reported as ng/mL change or concentration fold-change.

R2V COMPUTES NO OBSERVED S3/S4 RESULTS.
