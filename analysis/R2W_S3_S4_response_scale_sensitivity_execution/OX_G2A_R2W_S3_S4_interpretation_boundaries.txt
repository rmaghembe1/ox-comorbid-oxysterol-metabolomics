OX-COMORBID G2A R2W S3/S4 INTERPRETATION BOUNDARIES

STATUS
S3 and S4 are secondary response-scale sensitivity analyses.

VALUE SCALE
All results use internal-standard-normalized CPS-derived analytical response.
Subject response equals natural log(response_B / response_A).
Do not call these concentration fold-changes.

S3 HYPER
Holm-supported analytes: 24S_OHC, 25_OHC, 27_OHC, 7B_OHC, 7_KC.
The exact p-values use complete subject-level sign-flip reference distributions.
These are not design-based randomized-treatment tests.

S3 CONTROL
Holm-supported analytes: NONE.
The same response-scale and permutation limitations apply.

S4
Holm-supported between-block analytes: 24S_OHC, 25_OHC, 27_OHC, 7B_OHC, 7_KC.
S4 enumerates all C(20,10)=184756 group-label allocations.
Its p-values are conditional label-permutation references under exchangeability.

PROVENANCE
Clinical-group mapping remains PROVISIONAL_INDIRECT_HIGH_CONFIDENCE.
Baseline oxysterol information contributed to reconstruction of group identity.
The response ratio also contains baseline in its denominator.
Therefore S4 is not independent validation of the reconstructed clinical groups.
S3 group-specific sensitivity results are likewise interpreted conditional on the reconstructed block identities.

CAUSALITY
The hypercholesterolaemic block and intervention exposure are inseparable.
No S3 or S4 result establishes a randomized or causal simvastatin effect.

CONCENTRATION
No log response result is an ng/mL effect or authentic-analyte concentration fold-change.
